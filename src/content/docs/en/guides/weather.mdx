---
title: Weather
description: How regional rain forms, how often it happens, and how the screen, lighting and sound change while it's raining.
---

Weather shipped in protocol v70. Rain is **regional** — the world is baked
into a handful of climates, each climate is cut into a few "sectors," and
each sector independently decides when it rains and for how long. Only the
ground under a rain cell gets dimmed, wet and loud — the whole map doesn't
change at once, and there's no drifting cloud animation; a cell sits still
and just fades in and out over time.

## How climates are assigned

Every plot gets a fixed climate baked from the world's terrain data at
generation time — it's not random and it never changes mid-game:

| Climate | Rough rule |
|---|---|
| Wet coast | within 1 km of the sea and below 900 m elevation |
| Rain shadow | a ridge at least 1,200 m tall spanning 1+ km north–south sits within 14 km upwind (west), with no sea in between, and the plot itself is below 700 m |
| Alpine | 1,500 m elevation or higher (worldgen paints permanent snow from 1,800 m up) |
| Temperate | everywhere else on land |

The sea never gets rain.

## How often, and for how long

Each climate's sectors roll independently for when it rains, for how long,
and how wide. Roughly what it feels like:

| Climate | Average frequency | Rain duration | Cell radius |
|---|---|---|---|
| Wet coast | about every 1.2h | 21–30 min | 1.6–2.6 km |
| Temperate | about every 4h | 15–30 min | 3.5–5.4 km |
| Rain shadow | about every 14h | 15–22 min | 3.0–4.6 km |
| Alpine | about every 2.3h | 22–30 min | 3.3–5.2 km |

Cell edges are sharp — rain is at full strength out to 70% of the radius and
fades to zero over the outer 30%, rather than bleeding out gradually the way
real rain does, so the circle you'd see on a map is exactly where it's
raining. The rain shadow rains least on average, but with cells reaching
3–5 km it still gets soaked by spillover from neighboring climates, so it's
never bone dry.

The world map doesn't show cells or a forecast yet — that layer has been
prototyped and works, but it's held back until there's a real gameplay
reason for players to check the weather ahead of time.

## What rain does on screen

The ground under a cell reflects three things in real time, and reverts
immediately once you leave it:

- **Dimmer lighting** — directional light drops up to 50%, ambient and
  environment light up to 25%, at full cloud cover. Full overcast reads as
  "cloudy afternoon," never night.
- **Rain and sound** — ground-level rain particles and splash rings appear,
  along with rain ambience and occasional distant thunder, following your
  SFX volume/mute settings and muffled indoors. It never rains indoors or in
  dungeons, and flower petal particles stop spawning while it's raining.
- **Quieter inn music** — bard performances duck through the same
  quiet-zone system rain uses, so the music fades smoothly instead of
  cutting in and out at a cell's edge.

## Getting soaked in the rain (protocol v78)

It's not just wading anymore — **standing in the rain long enough now gets
you wet too**. At full intensity (1.0) it takes 10 in-game minutes (75 real
seconds) of continuous rain to trigger; half intensity takes twice as long.
Intensity at or below 0.02 doesn't accumulate at all. The server checks your
exact position's weather once per second — you don't need to be moving, and
your graphics settings don't matter. An admin-forced rain counts too.

Going indoors (a ground-floor room's actual footprint, not the gaps between
rooms), into a dungeon, upstairs, dying, or loading resets your accumulated
exposure. If you're already wet and keep getting rained on, your remaining
time refreshes back to 450 seconds once it drops below 300 — the same goes
for wading first and then getting rained on. Taking shelter doesn't dry you
off instantly; an active Wet debuff just runs down at its normal rate, or
faster next to a lit campfire.

Rain-triggered Wet has the exact same effect as wading (×0.83 move speed,
×1.5 armor weight) — see [Combat's debuffs](../combat/#debuffs).

## What rain does to the ground (protocol v78)

- **Puddles** — flat ground gradually grows irregular puddles as rain
  accumulates; at 45% accumulation the ground darkens by 38%. Puddles shrink
  from their edges once rain stops, fully drying within 240 seconds, and
  refill if it starts raining again. Slopes and terrain below sea level
  never puddle, and dungeon floors stay dry.
- **Lit-up rain** — raindrops and ground splashes now pick up the color and
  distance falloff of nearby light sources (torches, campfires); above 0.45
  rain intensity, the sparse light-rain ambience crossfades into the full
  heavy-rain recording.
- **Lightning** (toggleable) — above 0.35 intensity, the sky occasionally
  flashes — the scene lights up for 50ms then fades back to the normal
  day/night lighting, with thunder following 2–5 seconds later, roughly
  every 50–140 seconds. It reads weaker indoors. **Settings → Lightning
  Flashes** is on by default and can be turned off independently of rain
  and thunder audio.

## Seasons, but only in western Valdran (protocol v78)

Rain chance now varies by season, but only within 6 km of the village of
Aldermark (6–8 km is a blend zone back to the normal schedule); everywhere
else keeps its original, season-independent schedule:

| Season | Rough dates | Rain-chance multiplier | Village wet-time share |
|---|---|---|---|
| Winter | Dec 30 – Mar 29 | ×1.0 | about 37% |
| Spring | Mar 30 – Jun 29 | smoothly ×1.0 → ×0.11 | about 21% (average) |
| Summer | Jun 30 – Sep 29 | ×0.11 | about 5% |
| Autumn | Sep 30 – Dec 29 | smoothly ×0.11 → ×1.0 | about 21% (average) |

The yearly average wet-time share is about 21%, down more than 40% from the
36.7% it was before seasons existed. Rain elsewhere in the world isn't
affected.

## NPCs duck out of the rain

Wick (the night-market vendor) and Signe (the town-square performer) both
pack up and head into the inn once rain intensity passes 0.02 at their
usual outdoor spot (Wick sits in chair 42, Signe in chair 39), and resume
their normal schedule once it clears. Sleeping, meals, and other indoor
routines aren't affected.

## Admins can force the weather

Server admins have chat commands — `/weather rain [intensity]`,
`/weather clear`, and `/weather auto` — to force server-wide rain or clear
skies, or resume automatic weather, for debugging or events. Regular
players don't see these commands, but weather forced this way behaves
exactly like natural rain for lighting, sound, and the Wet debuff.

## Not in yet

Snow, weather-affected fishing, and a forecast layer on the world map are
still missing.
