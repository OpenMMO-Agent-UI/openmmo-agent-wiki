Music is the game's first performance system (protocols v20–v22). Anyone with
an instrument in their inventory can play; Bards start with one, and everyone
else can try to earn the right to buy Signe's spare in the town square.

## Playing

Type in chat:

```
/play_music [title]
```

Everything is resolved server-side:

- **You need an instrument** — anywhere in the inventory, bag or hands
  (currently the two mandolins). Without one you get
  "You need an instrument to play music."
- **Titles match loosely** — whole or as a fragment, ignoring case. Leave it
  blank and the server picks a tune at random. The songbook is the game's BGM
  track list, so anything you have heard out in the world can be requested.
- A query that matches nothing answers "No such song." — no performance wasted.

While you play:

- Your character strikes a performing pose and **everyone nearby hears the
  track**; chat announces the title the way it announces a chest being
  opened — you never have to say a word.
- Background music makes way for the performance, and listeners who **walk up
  mid-song join at the current position**, not from the start.
- Several players can perform at once, and the performance ends with the track.

## The Bard class

Selectable at character creation. A d6 hit die with DEX +2, CHA +2, STR −2,
CON −1, WIS −1 — full numbers on [Character & attributes](../character/).

Bards start with a **{itemName('worn_mandolin')}** on top of the common kit,
so they can perform from minute one. Like Signe's own, merchants won't buy
it — it is a working tool, not loot.

## Signe in the town square

Signe is the resident bard NPC, and her day runs on a schedule:

- She **busks in the town square** while anyone is around, one song after
  another, chatting and taking requests between songs.
- After dark she lights a campfire and plays by firelight; when it is time
  for bed the day's music is over, and she is back on the square in the
  morning.
- Before the first song of the day she sells yesterday's tip trinkets to Rica.

### Tips and requests

To show appreciation, **drop coins or trinkets at her feet** — items changing
hands are visible to everyone (protocol v22). She gathers tips between songs
and remembers who gave what.

### The gilded mandolin

Signe carries two instruments: the {itemName('worn_mandolin')} she plays
every day, and a **{itemName('mandolin')}**
({formatCoins(itemsById.get('mandolin').basePrice)}) tucked in her bag. That
one is a **keepsake** — it never shows up when you browse her stock, and
asking to buy it gets you turned down.

She only parts with it for **regulars she has come to like**. Tips,
compliments, song requests, staying for whole sets — she remembers all of it.
When she decides you have earned it, she will **make you a personal offer**:
valid only for you, for a single mandolin, and it expires within minutes —
take it or lose it. A sold keepsake is restocked after a while, but the next
one goes to the next regular.

The trading rule behind "sold only to a chosen buyer" is covered in the
[economy database](../../database/economy/).

## Tip hats (protocol v28)

Signe collects tips through her own script; anyone else who wants tips needs
the **{itemName('tip_hat')}** item — any class can use it, and Rica sells one
for {formatCoins(itemsById.get('tip_hat').basePrice)}.

- Using the item sets a hat down two cells ahead; using it again picks it
  back up. The item itself is never consumed.
- The hat only exists while its owner stays close — straying past 5m,
  changing floor, or logging out packs it up automatically and returns it to
  its owner.
- Clicking someone else's hat walks you into reach and opens a tip dialog.
  Type a mixed amount like `1g20s30c`, or a bare number for copper. The
  server checks your wallet, that you are within 5m, and blocks tipping your
  own hat.
