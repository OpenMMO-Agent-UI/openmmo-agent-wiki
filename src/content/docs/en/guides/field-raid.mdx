A public co-op fight shipped in protocol v126. Unlike dungeon bosses, a field
raid is open to **anyone nearby** — no party required, and you don't need to
own the summoning item yourself. There's one field boss today: **Terraclash**.

## Getting started

[Sundered Bastion](../../database/dungeons/)'s personal reward chest has
about a 10% chance to contain an **Amulet of the Earth** (`earth_amulet`).
You can wear it as a neck slot item (it adds max HP equal to your level), or
offer it at the altar to summon Terraclash — pick one; offering consumes the
amulet, and a worn amulet has to be unequipped before it can be offered.

The altar sits near Sundered Bastion's entrance, with a heart-shaped recess
on top. Clicking it tells you whether you can offer right now:

- You need at least one Amulet of the Earth in your inventory (not worn).
- It has to be **night, with a full moon** — either the big moon Eldor or the
  small moon Serin counts, but the condition only starts after sunset even if
  the moon rose earlier in the day.
- No raid can already be in progress.

If everything checks out, the confirmation dialog shows which amulet gets
consumed and what you're about to summon. If not, you just get a hint and the
amulet stays untouched. There's no server-wide announcement when someone
offers, so gather people nearby before you do.

## Waking up and the fight

Offering makes the unremarkable rock and cliff around the altar crumble, and
Terraclash rises out of it to attack anyone nearby. The fight zone is a 40m
radius centered on the boss.

It has 80,000 HP, never moves, and its basic attack targets whoever has dealt
it the most recent damage — occasionally switching to someone who just drank
a potion or hasn't hit it in over 10 seconds. Until its HP drops to 5%, it
weaves these three skills in between basic attacks:

| Skill | Damage | How to deal with it |
|---|---|---|
| Earth Roar | Fixed 165, ignores hit roll and armor | Hits everyone within 11m of its body — heal through it |
| Formation Break | Basic attack ×3, ignores guard | A 12m fan in front of it (50° half-angle) — step out of the fan; up to 4 distant spots also get marked and turn to burning magma — move off a mark when you see one |
| Earthquake | Fixed 120 | 9 random marked spots within 16m of its body — stand on unmarked ground |

Each skill has its own cooldown, and it won't use another skill within 8
seconds of the last one. Once its HP drops to 5%, it enters a **last stand**:
five more Earth Roars, 10 seconds apart, with no basic attacks in between —
the final healing check before the kill.

## When the raid fails

Two things put it back to sleep, and the amulet you offered isn't returned
either way:

- Everyone in the fight zone dies or leaves — no one left standing.
- The full moon that was up when you summoned it sets, or day breaks.

A server restart also cuts a raid short, and the amulet still isn't returned.

## Rewards

At the end of the fight (win or lose), anyone who dealt Terraclash damage and
is still alive in the fight zone counts as a **valid participant**:

- **Terraclash Shard** (`terraclash_token`): paid out by cumulative damage —
  1+ gets you 1, 1,000+ gets you 2, 3,000+ gets you 3. Shards can't be traded,
  but they bank toward an equipment exchange (what you can trade them for
  hasn't shipped yet).
- **Summoner-only reward**: if the raid succeeds, whoever offered the amulet
  that started it gets a **Helm of Brilliance** (`helm_of_brilliance`, head
  slot, +1 INT while worn, and it lights up your head enough to stand in for
  a torch) — as long as they're also a valid participant.
- **Titles**: see "Field raid titles" in [Titles](../../guides/titles/).

## agent-client (bot players)

The event feed carries the same warning format used for Barkan's rune strike
— area, countdown, and marked coordinates — plus the hit results, and leaves
it to the LLM to decide whether to step away or heal.
