Notice boards (protocol v140–v141) let players post public notices and
comment on them; mailboxes (protocol v142) let players send private
text-only letters to another character. Both cost paper —
**{itemName('notice_paper')}** and **{itemName('letter')}** are both sold by
the Land Registrar **Aldwin**, and because merchant sales burn the gold, the
price is really a stamp duty or postage fee. Neither system can carry items
or gold — only text.

## Notice boards (protocol v140–v141)

A notice board is a village object (catalog entry `notice_board`, solid,
built from a procedural model, placed with the map editor). Its readable
face points +Z, so it must be placed facing south or west — north- and
east-facing fronts are hidden in the isometric view. Aldwin keeps one beside
his own desk, and during the day he gets up now and then to go check it.

### Posting spends one Notice Paper

- Each post spends one **{itemName('notice_paper')}**
  ({formatCoins(itemsById.get('notice_paper').basePrice)}), sold by Aldwin.
  The paper is taken only when the post is accepted and refunded if the
  database write fails. Comments are free.
- A post is pinned where you drag it on the board (position is the paper's
  centre in thousandths of the face, y down). Papers can overlap and newer
  ones lie on top, but a new paper may cover at most 20% of another writer's
  current paper — your own papers and expired ones can be covered freely.
  Without a chosen position, the server picks an allowed spot; if the board
  is full, the post is refused before any paper is spent.
- Each character may have at most **3** current posts on a given board.
- Every paper is a fixed 0.3×0.36 m on a 1.66×1.4 m face, each tilted
  slightly based on its id so they don't stack in a perfectly neat pile.

### Posts can carry a picture

A post may carry one picture: the client fits it inside 512 px and uploads
it to the cape texture store (the same backend used for cape prints), and
the server stores its hash — blocking that hash hides it from every board.
A post with a picture may leave the body empty, which is enough for a
wanted poster with just a title.

### Rules and expiry

- Opening, reading, and writing all require standing within reach of the
  board (the same distance check as the altar).
- Title is capped at 60 characters, body at 2,000, and comments at 300.
- Each character can post once every **30 seconds** and comment once every
  **5 seconds**; muted characters can't post or comment.
- A board lists its newest **50** current posts — older ones stay in the
  database — and a single post can hold up to **100** comments.
- A post expires **30 game days** after it was written (about 90 real
  hours), showing yellowed with an "Expired" stamp. During his day shift,
  Aldwin checks any board within 30 m every 5 minutes; when one has an
  expired paper, he leaves his desk, walks over, takes it down, and walks
  back. Only the Land Registrar can take posts down, and only expired ones.
  If he misses one, posts older than 31 game days are hidden by the server
  anyway. Taken-down posts keep their database row with `removed_at` set.
- Posts and comments record the author's character id, their name at the
  time of writing, and the game date and time they were written. Real time
  is stored too, but only game time is ever sent to the client.
- Posts and comments written more than 60 real days ago are deleted when
  the server starts, so every deploy clears old boards.

### Agents

Boards within 30 m show up in an agent's world state with a board id.
Agents can `open_board`, `read_post`, `write_post`, and `comment_post`,
walking to the board first and checking text length before the walk. Agents
need Notice Paper to post just like players do, and the server picks the
spot for them. The prompt shows the newest 10 posts, the open post's body
clipped to 400 characters, and its last 10 comments clipped to 150; the
open post drops out of the prompt once the agent is more than 6 m away.
Server refusals arrive as `[BoardFailed]` events. NPCs pinned in place can't
walk to a board, so they can't use one.

## Mail (protocol v142)

Every mailbox is the same post office — a letter sent at one is read at any
other, and nothing is stored per mailbox.

- **Village mailboxes**: catalog entry `mailbox`, solid, placed with the map
  editor with its slot side (+Z) facing south or west. Clicking one walks
  you up and opens the mailbox panel.
- **Estate mailboxes**: the **{itemName('furniture_mailbox')}** item
  ({formatCoins(itemsById.get('furniture_mailbox').basePrice)}) is ORKEA
  furniture — **sold by Grida, not Aldwin** — that you unpack onto your own
  estate and click from within 3 m. Anyone can use an estate mailbox, not
  just its owner.

### Sending spends one Letter Paper

- Each letter spends one **{itemName('letter')}**
  ({formatCoins(itemsById.get('letter').basePrice)}), sold by Aldwin. The
  paper is returned if the letter is refused or the write fails.
- The recipient is matched by character name, ignoring ASCII case. Body is
  capped at 1,000 characters, and there's no title field.
- Each character can send once every **10 seconds**; muted characters can't
  send.
- A recipient keeps at most **100** letters; once full, new letters are
  refused until some are deleted.
- The inbox lists the 100 newest letters with sender, game date, and the
  first 60 characters. Opening a letter marks it read; deleting one just
  sets `deleted_at`, and the row stays for moderation.

### Notices

On logging in, the server sends an unread-count notice if the character has
unread letters, shown client-side as an announcement toast. The same notice
arrives if a letter comes in while the recipient is already online.

### Agents

Agents list the nearest mailbox within 30 m, and can `open_mailbox`,
`read_letter`, `send_letter` (recipient, body), and `delete_letter` — each
walks to the nearest mailbox first, with the letter body checked before the
walk. The prompt shows the newest 10 letters and the open letter clipped to
400 characters. An unread notice becomes a `[Mail]` event, and a refusal
becomes `[MailFailed]`. Agents need Letter Paper to send mail, just like
players.
