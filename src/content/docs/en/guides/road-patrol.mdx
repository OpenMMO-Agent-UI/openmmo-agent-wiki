Starting at the north gate of Aldermark, four guards — **Garrick**,
**Brenna**, **Osric**, and **Halda** — patrol in day and night pairs,
clearing [estate fences](../estates/#wooden-fences-protocol-v57) built on top
of public roads. Placing a fence is still unrestricted — only one that
actually blocks the road gets torn down (protocol v156).

## What counts as blocking

The server decides this on its own; you don't need to report anything. An
estate fence counts as a violation when its center sits within **3m** of a
worldgen road's centerline. Fences an operator placed outside any estate
don't count. Clearing is a guard-only `PatrolSweep` action — clearing
several fence segments under the same account in one sweep still counts as
**one violation**.

For the first 3 game days after the server starts (about 9 real hours),
guards only warn you instead of actually clearing fences. Enforcement
begins after that.

## Violations and buy-back

- The **first** violation gets your fence impounded. Pay half its base
  value to guard captain **{karl.npcName}** and you get everything back.
- The **second** violation onward is forfeit — no buy-back.
- Every fence cleared sends the owner a letter signed by Karl.

Right-click Karl and choose "Find impounded items" to open the buy-back
window, or just ask him in words — his LLM recognizes the request. If
you're standing near him it opens right away; if you're too far, Karl just
gets a note that you asked.

The route runs along Aldermark's north road, out past the Skeleton Crypt
fork to the Thieves' Den fork and back — about **1,145m** round trip, taking
roughly 2 game hours per lap. A lap interrupted (say, by the guard dying)
picks back up where it stopped; guards respawn at the guard station.

## The patrol guards

All four are level 50 with 700 max HP and Guard 24, wielding a rune blade.
Garrick and Osric wear plate armor, Brenna and Halda wear valkyrie armor,
and all of them wear a wool cape printed with the Aldermark crest
(protocol v157). A guard's name shows their **rank** in place of a family
name: Karl is captain, Garrick and Osric are sergeants, and Brenna and
Halda are guards.

Guards keep their weapon sheathed while walking. If a partner or player
within 20m gets into a fight with a monster, they join in against that
monster; within 40m, they run over to help. The weapon is sheathed again
10 seconds after the last hit.

Before clearing a fence, the guard assigned to it walks up and swings their
weapon three times first, then clears every qualifying fence within 24m at
once (protocol v158) — it isn't instant just because a guard walked past.

## Breaking out when penned

When a patrolling guard's movement gets stuck, it sends `PatrolBreakOut`
(protocol v160). The server floods outward from the guard's cell over every
cell it can walk to, treating doorways as open:

- Reaching a worldgen road, or getting **48 cells** away, means it isn't
  penned — nothing gets torn down.
- If the flood runs out of room on every side, every estate fence ringing
  that area is forfeited. Nested rings get torn through up to **8 layers**
  deep in one request.
- This seizure ignores the warning period and isn't covered by the
  [buy-back rule](#violations-and-buy-back) — it's forfeit outright, no
  buy-back — but the violation count still goes up by one and Karl's letter
  still goes out.
- A guard standing on furniture (a bed, a chair) doesn't count as penned,
  since the furniture itself walls in every side of its own cell; nothing
  gets torn down and the guard isn't sent back.
- If the guard is still penned after its fences are cleared (say, walled in
  by a house or an operator-placed fence), it's teleported straight back to
  town.
- Failing to reach the next patrol waypoint **three times** in a row sends
  `recall` instead, which sends the guard back to town regardless of
  whether it's penned; reaching one waypoint resets the count to zero.
  Scheduled moves like meals and sleep never trigger this.

## Notices

Karl posts a bilingual Korean/English notice on Aldermark's board under his
own name explaining the rule, refreshed automatically 5 game days before it
expires. See [Mail & notice boards](../mail-and-notices/).
