## How to start

Two routes:

- **Browser** — open [openmmo.to.nexus](https://openmmo.to.nexus) and sign in
  with Google. Nothing to install.
- **Desktop client** — [download OpenMMO Agent](../../client/download/). Adds
  multiple server profiles, encrypted key storage, and **letting an LLM drive
  your character** while you watch.

## Creating a character

**Pick a class first, then roll stats** — the order matters, because class
modifiers are applied on top of the dice.

Up to **3 characters** per account. You choose a name, class and gender.

The full modifier table and formulas are in
[Character and attributes](../character/). Three things to know:

- **The total is always 72**, whatever class you pick. Class decides the
  *shape* of your spread, not how much you get.
- **Want to survive hits?** Pick a d10 hit-die class: Knight, Barbarian,
  Caveman, Valkyrie.
- **The Tourist has a d4 hit die** — the squishiest thing in the game. Not
  recommended for a first character.

## What you start with

- **Money: 0** — no starting funds. Your first income comes from selling
  something.
- **Weapon: {itemName('worn_iron_sword')}** (1d6) — note it **has no price**, so
  merchants won't buy it. That's deliberate: it stops people rerolling
  characters to cash out starting gear.
- **Satiation: 700** — inside the Well-Fed band, so you have ×1.1 move and
  attack speed and ×1.15 carry weight.

:::tip["Worn" means unsellable]
Anything named "Worn" in this game **cannot be turned into money**, though its
stats match the real thing or come close. The
{itemName('worn_iron_sword')} is 1d6; the proper
{itemName('iron_sword')} is 1d8.
:::

## First job: earn some money

You have nothing, so you need to kill something and sell what it drops.

Start with the **Kobold** — level 1, Guard 8, and it **only fights back**, so it
won't charge you. Whether you fight is your call.

:::caution[Do not touch these at level 1]
The **Stalker** won't attack first, but it runs at 8.0 — your baseline is 3.0,
so provoking one means **you cannot get away.**
The **Orc Warlord** sits on the bottom floor of Orc Warrens with 120 health, +10
to hit and 2d8 damage. Level 1 has no chance.
:::

## Remember to eat

Satiation starts at 700 and drops **1 every 20 seconds while you're online**
(not offline). Below **300** you lose the Well-Fed buff; below **100** you go
Weak: move and attack speed ×0.75, carry ×0.6, and **HP regeneration stops
entirely.**

The good news is **you can't starve to death** — it stops at 0. But fighting
while Weak is a bad deal.

A game day (three real hours) burns 540, which is exactly one loaf of bread.
**The design target is one meal a day.**

Full comparison in [Food and satiation](../../database/food/).

## What happens when you die

- **You keep your equipment.**
- You lose **15% of the current level's XP band.**
- If that drops you below the level's starting XP you **lose a level** — but
  never more than one per death, and never at level 1.
- You respawn at the origin `(0, 0, 0)` at full health, with satiation reset to
  300.

Dying isn't cheap, but it isn't ruinous. Details in
[Combat](../combat/).

## Where to go next

1. **Kill Kobolds and Goblins** for gear and XP, and sell the weapons they drop
   to Rica.
2. **Find the Old Crypt** — the 5-floor tier-1 dungeon. The
   {itemName('leather_helmet')} is a guaranteed drop, and
   {itemName('leather_pants')} and {itemName('leather_belt')} are 30% each.
   Assemble the leather set (Guard 6 total).
3. **Then Orc Warrens** — 10 floors, filling in the leather chest, gloves and
   boots plus Iron Boots and an Iron Helmet. The boss, the Orc Warlord, is a
   guaranteed {itemName('iron_sword')} (1d8) — which no shop sells.
4. **Then Ogre Stronghold** — 15 floors, farther from Aldermark than Orc
   Warrens. The boss, the Ogre Warlord, is a guaranteed
   {itemName('greatclub')}. The chest guarantees {itemName('chain_mail')},
   with {itemName('iron_gauntlets')}, {itemName('plate_greaves')} and
   {itemName('plate_boots')} each at 37%, filling out the chain and plate
   pieces.

All three dungeons are near **Aldermark**; coordinates and distances are in
[World map](../../database/map/).

:::note[Dungeons are fixed]
Dungeons are seed-determined, so **the same entrance always has the same
layout.** Learn one and it becomes home ground. The world map records entrances
you've already found, but you have to walk to one the first time.
:::

## Things that catch people out

- **Hits must exceed, not match** — d20 plus your attack bonus landing exactly
  on the target's Guard is a **miss**.
- **The bag has a weight limit** (STR × 15, ×1.15 while Well-Fed). Going over
  doesn't stop you moving, but it **refuses new pickups and purchases.**
- **Anything dropped on the ground can be picked up by anyone.** It's
  floor-aware: something dropped on a house's second floor can only be
  retrieved from that floor.
- **Raw fish cause food poisoning** — five minutes of ×0.6 to everything with
  regeneration stopped. Grill it first.
- **Weapons never come from chests.** Want a particular weapon? Kill the monster
  carrying it.
