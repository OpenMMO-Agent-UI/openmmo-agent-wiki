title: The hair salon
description: How the hairdresser Odette changes a character's face, hair, hair colour and eye colour, what each change costs, and when she'll refuse.
---

If you want to change how your character looks after creation, find the
hairdresser Odette in Aldermark — she runs the Silver Comb Salon just north
of the tailor's shop (protocol v166). She only changes **face, hair style,
hair colour and eye colour** — gender, class and name never change.

## How it works

Walk into the salon and sit in one of the guest chairs — that opens the menu
automatically, **not clicking Odette**. The chair has to be within 6 metres
of Odette, and she has to be awake (not asleep for the night) for it to
work. Once you sit, Odette walks around to stand behind the chair and asks
what you'd like to change. The menu is the same appearance picker as the
character-creation screen; since the face is baked into the model, changing
it rebuilds your character model.

## Prices

Each part is **only charged if it actually changes** — untouched parts are
free. Switching a colour back to its "original" value counts as a change
too:

| Part | Price |
|---|---:|
| Face | 1 gold (10,000 copper) |
| Hair style | 50 silver (5,000 copper) |
| Hair colour (including reverting to original) | 20 silver (2,000 copper) |
| Eye colour (including reverting to original) | 20 silver (2,000 copper) |

If nothing actually changes, there's nothing to charge, and the request is
rejected as a no-op. The gold charge and the save happen in one
transaction — if the save fails, the gold is refunded.

## When she'll refuse

- You're **more than 6 metres** from Odette, or she's asleep.
- You're an **official NPC**, or your class has no appearance options to
  begin with (classes that skip the face/hair picker at creation).
- You're **currently trading with another player**.
- You **don't have enough gold** for what you asked to change.

## After you get up

When you leave the chair, Odette notices whether you actually changed
anything — a compliment if you did, an invitation to come back if you
didn't. Anyone nearby sees your new look right away, and so does anyone who
walks up later — they see you already changed.
