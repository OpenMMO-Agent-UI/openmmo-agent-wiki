A character with an unindebted estate can found a family at **Aldwin**, the
Land Registrar on Aldermark's town square (protocol v144). Every batch of
features added since then — chat, storage, notice boards (protocol v146),
emblems, banners, and mergers (protocol v147), family names shown in
whispers and the friend list (protocol v148) — builds on this same family.

:::note[Not a guild, not a house]
In the code this is called `family`; `guild` is the name used only by the
Shadow Guild dungeon and has nothing to do with player families, and
`house` refers specifically to housing. There's no war, alliance, or
ranking between families yet, and no family level, experience, or
dedicated treasury — only deposits into the tax account are tied to a
family permission.
:::

## Founding (protocol v144)

Talk to Aldwin and open "Family business"; with no family you'll see
"Found". Founding costs 10 **{itemName('revenue_stamp')}**
({formatCoins(itemsById.get('revenue_stamp').basePrice)} each from Aldwin,
10,000 copper total — a fifth of a Land Deed's 50,000). Stamps are
consumed from your bag, refunded if the request is refused, the same flow
as Notice Paper or Letter Paper. Aldwin can't handle any family business
while he's asleep.

The founder becomes **head**, and the founder's own unindebted estate is
automatically attached as the family's estate — founding fails if that
estate is already attached to another family (usually through another
character on the same account).

Family names follow the same rules as character names: ASCII
alphanumerics, `_`, and CJK characters, can't start with a digit or `_`,
same length limit, same banned-name list. Names are unique server-wide,
case-insensitively, and can't collide with any character name either.
Only the head can rename, once every 30 real days, for another 10 stamps.
Dissolving requires the head to be the only member left; it's done at
Aldwin's for 1 stamp, not from the family panel. A retired or pre-rename
name becomes reusable 30 days later; a name retired by a merge is never
reused.

Every member's nameplate, chat lines, and the character-select screen
append the family name after the character's own.

## Joining, leaving, and kicking

A member with invite permission can invite any online, family-less
character; the invite lives in memory and expires after 120 seconds, the
same as a friend request. Official NPCs can't be invited. Families cap
out at **30 members**.

- Any member but the head can leave at any time.
- A member with kick permission and a higher rank than the target can kick
  them.
- Leaving or being kicked locks you out of joining a new family for **24
  hours** — enough to stop someone looting the shared storage and
  hopping families.
- The head can only leave after handing off headship.
- Deleting a character counts as leaving; if it was the head, the
  succession order below takes over automatically.

## Ranks and permissions (protocol v144)

Ranks are fixed at four tiers; the titles are the family's own:

| Rank | Default title | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Head | Exactly 1, every permission, can't be changed |
| 1 | Elder | |
| 2 | Family | New members default here |
| 3 | Guest | |

The head toggles these permissions individually for ranks 1–3: invite
(`INVITE`), kick (`KICK`), build (`BUILD_HOUSE`), demolish
(`DEMOLISH_HOUSE`), place furniture and banners (`PLACE_FURNITURE`),
landscape and fences (`LANDSCAPE`), family-chest deposit
(`STORAGE_DEPOSIT`) and withdraw (`STORAGE_WITHDRAW`), board-write
(`BOARD_WRITE`) and take-down (`BOARD_MODERATE`), treasury deposit
(`TREASURY_DEPOSIT`), emblem editing (`EDIT_EMBLEM`), and rank/permission
editing (`EDIT_RANKS`). Reading the board and family chat are open to
every member regardless of permissions.

Succession: the head can directly hand headship to another member.
Deleting the head's character auto-promotes whoever is first in the
succession order — highest rank first, then earliest to join. If the head
goes 30 days without logging in, whoever is first in the succession order
can claim headship; if they're also gone 30 days, the claim passes to the
next in line.

## Sharing land (protocol v144)

A family doesn't own estates — ownership stays with the account, and the
family only overlays access on top. An estate attaches to at most one
family at a time; a member who owns an estate and belongs to that family
can attach or detach it. Merging (see "Merging families" below) attaches
both families' estates to the new family, with no separate cap on estate
count — since every account already gets only one estate, the 30-member
cap is the real ceiling.

Once attached, these checks that used to require the same account now
also accept the matching family permission:

- Building and demolishing — family permission `BUILD_HOUSE` /
  `DEMOLISH_HOUSE`.
- Placing, moving, and recovering furniture, and editing sign text —
  family permission `PLACE_FURNITURE`.
- Fences and landscaping — family permission `LANDSCAPE`.
- Treasury deposits — family permission `TREASURY_DEPOSIT`; **withdrawing
  still requires the owning account alone**.

[Estate chests](../estates/#estate-storage-protocol-v61) aren't covered by
family permissions at all — they're still placed and opened by the
estate's owning account only, since chest access is checked against the
estate's account and placing one on someone else's estate would hand its
contents to that estate's owner. An indebted estate stays off-limits to
family members, exactly as it does to the owner. A seized estate detaches
from its family; the family itself isn't dissolved, it just loses the
building, banner, board, and storage features tied to that estate.

### Seat and the Scroll of Family Return (protocol v147)

The head can pick a **seat** from the family panel's list of attached
estates; without a choice it defaults to the head's own estate, and
failing that, the first estate ever attached. Rica and Wick both sell a
**{itemName('scroll_of_family_return')}**
({formatCoins(itemsById.get('scroll_of_family_return').basePrice)}), which
takes you to the seat — landing on a hearthbound rug if one is placed
there, otherwise safe outdoor ground. The scroll isn't consumed if there's
no family, the seat has no estate attached, or the seat is indebted. The
regular [Scroll of Estate Return](../estates/#buying-a-deed-and-claiming-land-protocol-v55)
is unaffected and still returns you to your own estate.

## Family chat (protocol v146)

`/g` (or the chat channel menu) speaks to every online family member, with
blocks and mutes still applying. Members coming online, going offline,
joining, leaving, or taking over headship all post a system line to the
family channel.

## Family chest (protocol v146)

Aldwin sells a **{itemName('family_chest')}**
({formatCoins(itemsById.get('family_chest').basePrice)}), placeable only on
an estate attached to a family, capped at **4 per family**, holding **200
kg** each (versus 50 kg for a regular
[estate chest](../estates/#estate-storage-protocol-v61)). Placement
records which family the estate belongs to at that moment, and the owner
is recorded as the estate's owner rather than whoever placed it — so the
chest survives even if the placer later leaves or is deleted.

- Opening requires standing within 3 m and belonging to that family.
- Depositing needs `STORAGE_DEPOSIT`, withdrawing needs
  `STORAGE_WITHDRAW` — **the estate's own owner can't reach the contents
  without the matching rank either**; the chest belongs to the whole
  family, not the estate's owner.
- Every deposit and withdrawal is logged; the family panel's "Family
  storage log" shows the newest 200.
- Recovering a chest only works while it's empty.
- An estate can't detach from its family while its family chests still
  hold anything.
- An estate that detaches through a kick or seizure leaves its chests
  withdraw-only, until a member empties them and the estate's owner
  recovers them.
- Once a family dissolves, only the last head can withdraw whatever is
  left in its chests.

## Family notice board (protocol v146)

Aldwin sells a **{itemName('family_board')}**
({formatCoins(itemsById.get('family_board').basePrice)}), placed on a
family estate and readable, writable, and commentable only by that
family's members. Unlike the
[village notice board](../mail-and-notices/), writing costs no Notice
Paper and posts **never expire** — instead a board caps out at 50 posts,
pushing out the oldest. Taking down a post needs `BOARD_MODERATE`
(or being the writer). Posts stay on a board that detaches from its
family, but reading and writing are both locked.

## Family emblem, capes, and banners (protocol v147)

A member with `EDIT_EMBLEM` can set the family emblem from the panel's
"Emblem" tab: a color plus a picture uploaded through the same pipeline as
cape prints (320×512, with the same upload limits, reporting, and hash
blocking — a blocked picture shows only the color). Once set, every
member's worn cape shows the family emblem and color in the world and on
the character-select screen, overriding the cape's own dye and print;
leaving the family restores the original look. Each member can toggle
"show the family emblem on my cape" off individually (on by default).

ORKEA's furniture shop gained two emblem pieces, also placeable only on an
estate attached to a family:

- **{itemName('family_banner')}**
  ({formatCoins(itemsById.get('family_banner').basePrice)}) — free-standing.
- **{itemName('family_pennant')}**
  ({formatCoins(itemsById.get('family_pennant').basePrice)}) — wall-mounted,
  raised up the wall like a display sword.

Both pieces get their picture filled in by the server from whichever
family the estate is currently attached to, rather than storing it on the
furniture itself; an estate leaving the family turns either one back into
bare, emblem-less linen.

## Merging families

### Terms and effects

Heads can merge two families. The proposer picks which name, emblem, and
head survive (one from each side), and the other head accepting completes
it; both heads must be online, a proposal expires after 120 seconds, and a
refused proposal can't be resent until it expires. The family whose name
is kept absorbs the other's members, estates, furniture, and storage log,
and the absorbed family's row is deleted outright; a merge that would push
the combined membership past 30 is refused. The absorbed head is demoted
to **Elder**, everyone else keeps their rank, and rank titles and
permissions follow whichever family's name was kept. A retired name is
never reused. **A merge can't be undone** — the accept screen lists
everything that will change.

### The merger rite (protocol v149)

As of protocol v149, merging is only possible through a **family merger
rite** held at the **Two Moons Chapel** — the family panel's old direct
"merge with another family" proposal button has been hidden. Aldermark's
Two Moons Chapel honors Eldor, the elder moon (standing for keeping watch)
and Serin, the lesser moon (standing for moving forward); the faith holds
the world whole only when both moons share the sky, and its symbol is two
interlocking rings. The chapel is kept by priest **Maelis**.

Holding the rite requires Maelis to be awake and not sitting, with both
heads on the same floor within 8 m of her, or the proposal is refused. The
server runs the whole sequence:

1. One head proposes.
2. Maelis opens the rite, reciting the conditions.
3. 8 seconds later, the proposing head gets a consent prompt, answered
   with `ConfirmFamilyMerge`.
4. Once accepted, 6 seconds later the other head gets a consent prompt,
   answered with `RespondFamilyMerge`.
5. Accepting completes the merge and announces it server-wide.

The rite ends if either head refuses, walks away before answering, or 120
seconds pass. Maelis's lines are LLM-generated — the server sends her
alone a step-by-step `MergerRiteCue` — but only the server decides whether
the merge succeeds; her lines and the announcement are gender-neutral and
never call it a wedding.

## Whispers, party, and the friend list (protocol v148)

Whisper messages carry the sender's and recipient's family name, and your
own whisper echo shows the other side's family. Party chat lines and
party roster entries also carry a family name — the roster uses a live
family for nearby party members and the family from login time for
distant ones. The friend list and the online-friends query likewise look
up and show family: online friends update live, while an offline friend's
family is a snapshot from your last login and only refreshes the next
time you log in. Family-channel login/logout notices now also name the
member.
