---
title: Inn & maids
description: Where death respawns you, how sitting works, and how the maids Miriel and Cocoly's service triggers.
---

The inn is where death respawns you, and today the only place you can **sit
down**. Two maids, Miriel (protocol v46) and Cocoly (protocol v48), work the
inn's ground floor and greet players who just respawned or just sat down.

## Respawning

Dying no longer sends you back to the town spawn point — you land in the
inn's sick room instead (protocol v46):

- The sick room has **4 beds**. The server picks one that's free and poses
  the character lying in it.
- If all 4 are taken, the character stands beside the beds instead of
  clipping into anyone.
- Bed positions follow the furniture placed in the map editor, so moving the
  furniture moves the respawn point.
- Starting protocol v47, a maid recognizes a player who just respawned in a
  bed and walks over to greet them; the bed respawn itself already worked
  before that, the maid just wasn't showing up for it yet.

## Sitting

Any chair marked with the "sit" interaction in the furniture catalog can be
clicked to sit (protocol v46):

- Clicking a chair plays a sit-down animation; chatting while seated switches
  to a seated chat pose.
- Standing up plays a stand-up clip first, then the character steps to the
  free cell beside the chair — the cell directly in front of a chair is
  usually a table, so it steps aside instead of into it.
- Clicking the same chair again mid-walk no longer instantly pops the
  character back up (fixed in protocol v48).

## Maid service

Sitting on a chair near a maid queues a visit (protocol v48): she walks over
(some chairs have a curated stand spot in the data, others get a computed
one) and greets you for your order.

Miriel and Cocoly share one **visit claim board** — whether it's a bedside
respawn greeting or a seated guest, only the maid who claims a given bed or
player first will answer it, so you never get double-greeted or skipped
entirely.

Like other shop NPCs, the maids never open a trade window on their own — you
have to click them first before the trade UI appears (protocol v48).
