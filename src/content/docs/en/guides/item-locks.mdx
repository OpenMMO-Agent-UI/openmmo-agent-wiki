---
title: Item locks
description: Lock items to stop yourself from dropping or selling them by accident — locking also blocks trading, stalls, enchanting and batch actions; unlocking needs a typed confirmation.
---

The bag panel has a **Lock** button next to **Select** (protocol v69). Turning it on shows a
padlock on every slot in your bag and equipment, and clicking one locks that item. Turning
**Lock** off again, switching to **Select**, or closing the bag leaves only the padlocks on
already-locked items visible. **The yellow padlock on a locked item is always clickable to
start unlocking it, whether or not Lock mode is on.** Lock state also shows up in the item
tooltip.

If you're holding more than one of the same item, the padlock only affects the slot or stack
you click, not the whole quantity. Turning Lock mode on cancels any batch-drop selection you
had going.

## Unlocking needs a typed confirmation

Clicking the padlock on a locked item opens a dialog listing the item's name, enchant level and
quantity, and asks you to type **UNLOCK** in capitals before you can press **Unlock**. Cancel or
Esc keeps the item locked, and you'll need to type the confirmation again next time. If the item
in that slot changes while the dialog is open, the dialog closes on its own.

## What locking blocks

- **Single and batch drops, selling to a shop, putting the item into a player trade, and
  listing it on a stall** are all refused by the server. For batch actions, one locked item in
  the batch refuses the whole request. See [Player trading](../trade/#what-can-never-be-traded).
- **A locked weapon can't be picked by an enchant scroll, and locked armor is excluded from the
  random enchant-armor target.** If nothing enchantable is left, neither the scroll nor the
  whetstone oil is consumed. See [Enchanting](../enchant/).
- An item already on the trade table or listed on a stall has to be pulled back before you can
  change its lock, and both trades and stall sales re-check locks again at the moment they
  finalize.

## What locking doesn't block

- **Equipping, unequipping, and normal use** all work as usual.
- Using a locked **scroll or whetstone oil itself** isn't blocked — the lock only protects the
  item a scroll or oil would act on, not the consumable being used.

## Locks travel with the item

- Locks are preserved when you deposit into or withdraw from a storage chest.
- Two stacks with different lock states **won't merge**.
- Locks survive reconnecting. Items that existed before protocol v69 shipped start **unlocked**.

## Ground transfer audit log

The server logs successful single and batch drops and pickups of ordinary items to an
`item_audit` record (failed requests leave no record; coin pouches keep their existing pickup
log). Each entry records the character, item type and enchant, the actual quantity moved, the
drop/pickup location and floor, and who dropped it. This log is for operators auditing the
game — there's no in-game UI for it.
