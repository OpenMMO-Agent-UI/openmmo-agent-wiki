## Buying a deed and claiming land (protocol v55)

Aldwin sells **{itemName('land_deed')}** for a flat
{formatCoins(itemsById.get('land_deed').basePrice)}, no haggling. Buying and
carrying a deed has no level requirement, but **using one requires character
level 10+**.

Double-click a deed in your bag while standing on the plot you want, and the
game shows a terrain-following grid for your current 32×32m cell plus a "Claim
this plot?" confirmation:

- The server checks the claim conditions as soon as the preview opens — if
  they aren't met, the grid turns red with a rejection reason and the claim
  button is hidden.
- Moving while the preview is open refreshes the current cell and its
  eligibility about 0.35s after you stop; the claim button is disabled while
  that refresh is in flight.
- Confirming re-checks every condition server-side; on success it registers
  the plot and consumes one deed in the same transaction. Cancelling or
  failing never consumes the deed — a failure shows a red grid with the
  reason, a success shows green.

Zoom in on the world map to see land grade and ownership as color overlays:
crown land and reserved land (towns, dungeon entrances, and the sea) are
filled blocks, homestead plots show only grid lines. Your own plots use an
accent color, plots up for auction get a flag icon with a countdown, and
unpaid/neglected/ruined plots fade your ownership color by stage. **Other
players' plots each get one of four colors** (2026-09-20), assigned by a
graph-coloring algorithm so adjacent owners never share a color, making
boundaries easier to read at a glance.

Once you own a plot, both Rica and Wick sell a
**{itemName('scroll_of_estate_return')}**
({formatCoins(itemsById.get('scroll_of_estate_return').basePrice)}), the same
price as the regular return scroll. Using one sends your character to safe
outdoor ground on your own homestead — it works from a dungeon or mid-combat,
too. The server picks a spot near the center of your earliest-claimed plot
that avoids buildings, obstacles, and water deeper than 0.1m, falling back to
your other owned plots if none is found there. It isn't consumed if you have
no estate, are dead, have a pending trade, or no safe spot can be found, and
it can't send you to a plot owned by another character on the same account.

## Plot limits and expansion

Each **account** (not each character) can own one homestead estate. Claiming a
new cell merges it into your existing estate automatically as long as it
shares an edge with it, up to a cap of **16 cells**. You can't claim a
disconnected second plot on the same account — that needs a different
account.

## Tax account (protocol v56)

Clicking Aldwin now opens a dedicated **Real Estate** screen instead of a
general shop — he won't buy or haggle over anything else. The left side shows
your deed; below it, your tax account balance, next due date (in-game date
plus a live countdown), the amount coming due, and whether you're overdue.

- Click your carried gold to **deposit** into the tax account, or the account
  balance to **withdraw**. Amounts accept `1g20s30c`-style input or a plain
  copper number. Deposits and withdrawals commit in the same database
  transaction as your character's gold and bag, so nothing gets lost
  mid-transfer.
- Land tax is **20 silver per plot per in-game month** — a full 16-plot estate
  costs 3.2 gold a month.
- Tax is collected at **midnight on the first of the in-game big month**,
  roughly every 3.75 real days. **The first payment is waived.**
- The account balance has no cap, so you can pre-fund it before a long break.

### What happens if you fall behind

The decay stages shown on screen are currently a **design baseline**, not
fully enforced yet: missing your last login for 8 consecutive due dates
(roughly 30 real days) counts as unpaid regardless of balance, and every
further missed date adds to a running overdue count meant to step through
"overdue," "neglected," "ruined," and "reclaimed." The actual building and
furniture restrictions, ruin rendering, and cleanup on reclamation (deleting
the house, mailing furniture back) are **not implemented yet**. Depositing the
full arrears plus one extra month instantly restores good standing and waives
the next payment.

## Wooden fences (protocol v57)

{itemName('wooden_fence')} ({formatCoins(itemsById.get('wooden_fence').basePrice)},
bought from Rowan in whatever quantity you ask for) are one-meter fence
sections you place and recover along your estate's cell edges. They stack, so
even a hundred-plus takes one bag slot.

- Double-click a fence in your bag to enter placement mode: your bag closes
  and any queued move is cancelled. The whole estate shows a faint,
  terrain-following 1m dotted grid, independent of your mouse or camera —
  it disappears when you leave the mode.
- Hover the ground and the nearest 1m cell edge previews the real fence
  model: **green** means you can place it, **orange** means you can recover
  it, **red** means you can't touch it.
- Click an empty edge to spend one fence and place it; click a placed fence to
  remove it and refund one to your bag. The next click is ignored until the
  server responds, and your bag count follows the server's answer.
- Right-click to move without leaving placement mode; left-click places or
  recovers. Esc or Done exits — the mode stays active even at zero fences left
  so you can still recover what's already down.
- Outside the mode, right-clicking a placed fence within 5m re-enters it, even
  with an empty bag after relogging.

Placing and recovering follow different rules:

- **Placing** requires at least one of the edge's two neighboring cells to be
  your own plot, with **no unpaid tax**; edges on your estate's outer boundary
  are allowed too. Terrain-wise, water deeper than 0.1m or a height difference
  over 0.5m between the endpoints or midpoint blocks placement.
- **Recovering** only works for the plot's current owner, even while overdue
  on tax. If your bag is too heavy to take it, the fence stays put instead of
  being force-fit into your bag.

## Estate landscaping (protocol v58)

Buy a **{itemName('landscaping_toolbox')}** from Rowan
({formatCoins(itemsById.get('landscaping_toolbox').basePrice)}) and use it
once from your bag to open the landscaping panel — the toolbox itself is not
consumed and can be reused any time. The panel now bundles four tabs (as of
protocol v62, fences and storage chests share one "Objects" tab, and the
request to enter a tab was unified into a single message the server
authorizes per-tab):

- **Ground**: the existing splat-brush material painter.
- **Path**: the existing road tool, painting a path between two points.
- **Objects**: place or recover wooden fences, and place an
  [estate storage chest](#estate-storage-protocol-v61).
- **House**: place or demolish a
  [house built from a house scroll](#house-building-protocol-v60).

(A planting tab for grass, flowers, and saplings isn't built yet.)

The toolbox comes with two base materials built in — **Meadow Grass** and
**Rocky Trail** — paintable as soon as you buy it. Six more landscaping
palettes are sold separately, each
{formatCoins(itemsById.get('landscaping_palette_sand').basePrice)}, also from
Rowan; using one permanently unlocks its material so you never buy it again:
{itemName('landscaping_palette_sand')}, {itemName('landscaping_palette_red_soil')},
{itemName('landscaping_palette_gravel')}, {itemName('landscaping_palette_pebbles')},
{itemName('landscaping_palette_stone_path')}, and {itemName('landscaping_palette_paving')}.
Unlocks are bound to the character; a palette you've already learned is never
consumed again.

Brush and path widths are chosen in 1-meter steps, which the server converts
to an actual 0.5–10m radius (as of protocol v59 the radius supports fractions,
so even the narrowest one-cell brush lines up cleanly with the grid). Painting
only ever affects your own land — a brush that overlaps someone else's or
unclaimed ground only applies to the part inside your plot, and a path only
paints the stretch that runs through your own land. Admin characters skip the
ownership and boundary checks when editing ground or paths, but still need to
be carrying the toolbox, have the material unlocked, and be alive like anyone
else.

Terrain edits clear grass and trees under the existing rules, but repainting
the ground does **not** grow cleared vegetation back — that's what the future
planting tab is for.

## House building (protocol v60)

Rowan sells 5 fixed house scrolls for building on your own estate:

- **{itemName('scroll_of_small_house')}** ({formatCoins(itemsById.get('scroll_of_small_house').basePrice)}) — a small house, previewable at Karl's home.
- **{itemName('scroll_of_medium_house')}** ({formatCoins(itemsById.get('scroll_of_medium_house').basePrice)}) — a medium house, previewable at Rowan's home.
- **{itemName('scroll_of_small_two_story_house')}** ({formatCoins(itemsById.get('scroll_of_small_two_story_house').basePrice)}) — a small two-story house, previewable at Aldwin's home.
- **{itemName('scroll_of_medium_two_story_house')}** ({formatCoins(itemsById.get('scroll_of_medium_two_story_house').basePrice)}) — a medium two-story house, previewable at Rica's shop.
- **{itemName('scroll_of_large_two_story_house')}** ({formatCoins(itemsById.get('scroll_of_large_two_story_house').basePrice)}) — a large two-story house, previewable at the Aldermark Inn.

Pick a house scroll from your bag on the toolbox's **House** tab to open a
translucent preview; clicking to confirm has the server re-check that the
spot is within 30m, on dry ground, has no more than 1m of height difference
across the footprint, and doesn't overlap an existing house before it
consumes the scroll and spawns the house. The same tab lets you click your
own house to demolish it — no materials are refunded.

## Estate storage (protocol v61)

**{itemName('storage_chest')}** ({formatCoins(itemsById.get('storage_chest').basePrice)})
opens the placement UI straight from your bag when used. It can only go on an
estate you own with no back taxes, and inside a building it's placed on
whatever floor you're currently standing on. There's one capacity tier right
now, **50kg**, and no cap on how many chests an estate can hold. As of
protocol v89 it's sold from Grida's [ORKEA furniture shop](#orkea-furniture-shop-protocol-v87v89)
instead of by Aldwin.

The storage screen reuses the trade window's two-column layout — chest on the
left, your bag on the right — with both sides showing the resulting weight
live. Bound or untradeable items, and chests themselves, can't go in. An
empty chest can be picked back up: it returns to your bag if there's room, or
drops on the ground if not. If the estate lapses, chests are left behind and
anyone can open them. A house holding a chest can't be demolished until every
chest on every floor has been emptied and recovered.

## ORKEA furniture shop (protocol v87–v89)

A showroom near town at `world(-1450.5, 1.0, 4775.5)` sells 23 decorative
estate items: a bed, rustic bed, chair, table, chest, barrel, crate, stone
hearth, wall torch, signpost, four shop-sign variants, and potion, scroll,
and sword ornaments. Potions, scrolls, and sword ornaments cost 200 copper
each, the wall torch 300, the crate 500, the barrel 600, chairs and signs 800
each, the chest 900, the table 3000, the bed and rustic bed 4000 each, and
the stone hearth 12000. Decorative items have no real function — ornamental
potions and scrolls don't consume, ornamental weapons can't be equipped, and
the ornamental chest has no storage (buy the
{itemName('storage_chest')} above for that).

The shop's clerk, a female orc named **Grida**, greets customers at the
west end of the shop from 06:30–18:00 and 18:30–midnight. Click her to open
a trade window: the left-hand sell list shows every item for direct
purchase, or click a display within 3m to add it to the cart — either way
the quantities add up and matching items merge. `Confirm` checks your
distance, gold, and carry weight, then commits the whole order at once.
Grida also buys back priced, unlocked items at **40%** of base price like
Rica does; selling and buying furniture share one cart, with selling
processed first so a failed furniture purchase never undoes a completed
sale.

Use bought furniture directly, or place it on your active estate on your
current floor after selecting it under `Estate Editor → Objects` in
`Select` mode. Left-click or `Enter` places it; the rotate buttons or
`R`/`Shift + R` turn it — beds, chairs, tables, wall torches, and shop signs
snap to 90°, and arrow keys nudge 5cm north/south or east/west. Small
ornaments can sit on tabletops, with the mouse wheel or a height slider
raising them up to 3m off the floor. Already-placed furniture uses the same
`Select` mode to reposition, reorient, or re-height it at any distance,
showing a blue selection box while selected; `Recover` returns it to your
bag, dropping it on the ground if there's no room. A selected sign can be
edited in a `Sign text` field and saved with `Save text`, up to 120
characters — recovering a sign doesn't keep its text.

Sleeping on a placed **bed** or **rustic bed** for 16 consecutive seconds
doubles HP and MP natural regen — see
[Combat](../combat/#skills-and-mana-protocol-v71v79).
