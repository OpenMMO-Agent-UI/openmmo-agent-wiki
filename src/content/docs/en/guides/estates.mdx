**Aldwin**, the Land Registrar stationed on Aldermark's town square, is the whole
entry point to the land system for now — buying a deed, claiming a plot, and
managing your tax account all go through him (protocol v55, v56).

:::note[Still unfinished]
Only **homestead** plots can be bought and claimed right now. Crown land (the
ring near towns and dungeon entrances, meant for auctions, tournaments, and
merit grants) has no acquisition path yet. Claimed land can't hold a house or
furniture yet — only fences. Whether unpaid tax actually reclaims land is
covered under "Tax account" below.
:::

## Buying a deed and claiming land (protocol v55)

Aldwin sells **{itemName('land_deed')}** for a flat
{formatCoins(itemsById.get('land_deed').basePrice)}, no haggling. Buying and
carrying a deed has no level requirement, but **using one requires character
level 10+**.

Double-click a deed in your bag while standing on the plot you want, and the
game shows a terrain-following grid for your current 32×32m cell plus a "Claim
this plot?" confirmation:

- The server checks the claim conditions as soon as the preview opens — if
  they aren't met, the grid turns red with a rejection reason and the claim
  button is hidden.
- Moving while the preview is open refreshes the current cell and its
  eligibility about 0.35s after you stop; the claim button is disabled while
  that refresh is in flight.
- Confirming re-checks every condition server-side; on success it registers
  the plot and consumes one deed in the same transaction. Cancelling or
  failing never consumes the deed — a failure shows a red grid with the
  reason, a success shows green.

Zoom in on the world map to see land grade and ownership as color overlays:
crown land and reserved land (towns, dungeon entrances, and the sea) are
filled blocks, homestead plots show only grid lines. Your own plots use an
accent color, plots up for auction get a flag icon with a countdown, and
unpaid/neglected/ruined plots fade your ownership color by stage.

## Plot limits and expansion

Each **account** (not each character) can own one homestead estate. Claiming a
new cell merges it into your existing estate automatically as long as it
shares an edge with it, up to a cap of **16 cells**. You can't claim a
disconnected second plot on the same account — that needs a different
account.

## Tax account (protocol v56)

Clicking Aldwin now opens a dedicated **Real Estate** screen instead of a
general shop — he won't buy or haggle over anything else. The left side shows
your deed; below it, your tax account balance, next due date (in-game date
plus a live countdown), the amount coming due, and whether you're overdue.

- Click your carried gold to **deposit** into the tax account, or the account
  balance to **withdraw**. Amounts accept `1g20s30c`-style input or a plain
  copper number. Deposits and withdrawals commit in the same database
  transaction as your character's gold and bag, so nothing gets lost
  mid-transfer.
- Land tax is **20 silver per plot per in-game month** — a full 16-plot estate
  costs 3.2 gold a month.
- Tax is collected at **midnight on the first of the in-game big month**,
  roughly every 3.75 real days. **The first payment is waived.**
- The account balance has no cap, so you can pre-fund it before a long break.

### What happens if you fall behind

The decay stages shown on screen are currently a **design baseline**, not
fully enforced yet: missing your last login for 8 consecutive due dates
(roughly 30 real days) counts as unpaid regardless of balance, and every
further missed date adds to a running overdue count meant to step through
"overdue," "neglected," "ruined," and "reclaimed." The actual building and
furniture restrictions, ruin rendering, and cleanup on reclamation (deleting
the house, mailing furniture back) are **not implemented yet**. Depositing the
full arrears plus one extra month instantly restores good standing and waives
the next payment.

## Wooden fences (protocol v57)

{itemName('wooden_fence')} ({formatCoins(itemsById.get('wooden_fence').basePrice)},
also bought from Aldwin in whatever quantity you ask for) are one-meter fence
sections you place and recover along your estate's cell edges. They stack, so
even a hundred-plus takes one bag slot.

- Double-click a fence in your bag to enter placement mode: your bag closes
  and any queued move is cancelled. The whole estate shows a faint,
  terrain-following 1m dotted grid, independent of your mouse or camera —
  it disappears when you leave the mode.
- Hover the ground and the nearest 1m cell edge previews the real fence
  model: **green** means you can place it, **orange** means you can recover
  it, **red** means you can't touch it.
- Click an empty edge to spend one fence and place it; click a placed fence to
  remove it and refund one to your bag. The next click is ignored until the
  server responds, and your bag count follows the server's answer.
- Right-click to move without leaving placement mode; left-click places or
  recovers. Esc or Done exits — the mode stays active even at zero fences left
  so you can still recover what's already down.
- Outside the mode, right-clicking a placed fence within 5m re-enters it, even
  with an empty bag after relogging.

Placing and recovering follow different rules:

- **Placing** requires at least one of the edge's two neighboring cells to be
  your own plot, with **no unpaid tax**; edges on your estate's outer boundary
  are allowed too. Terrain-wise, water deeper than 0.1m or a height difference
  over 0.5m between the endpoints or midpoint blocks placement.
- **Recovering** only works for the plot's current owner, even while overdue
  on tax. If your bag is too heavy to take it, the fence stays put instead of
  being force-fit into your bag.
