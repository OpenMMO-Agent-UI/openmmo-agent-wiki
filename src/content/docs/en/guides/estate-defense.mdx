Shipped in protocol v182 — a **war horn**, a rare hunting drop, blown at
night on clear ground on your own (or your family's) estate calls five
waves of monsters from all sides. Kills pay exactly like any ordinary
monster; holding the banner is what earns the extra chest and trophy. It's
a small-scale event scoped to one estate and one family, not a town-wide
affair.

## Getting a war horn

War horns (`war_horn_1` through `war_horn_4`) are a world drop from monster
kills in the wild or in dungeons, at roughly **0.025%** (about one per 4,000
kills). Which grade drops is set by **the level of the monster you killed**:

| Grade | Item | Drops from kills leveled | Base price |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| Lesser | **{itemName('war_horn_1')}** | 1–12 (outdoors, shallow dungeons) | {formatCoins(itemsById.get('war_horn_1').basePrice)} |
| — | **{itemName('war_horn_2')}** | 13–25 (outdoors far from town, tier 3–5 dungeons) | {formatCoins(itemsById.get('war_horn_2').basePrice)} |
| Greater | **{itemName('war_horn_3')}** | 26–35 ([the Garden of Stone](../../database/dungeons/), floors 1–15) | {formatCoins(itemsById.get('war_horn_3').basePrice)} |
| Supreme | **{itemName('war_horn_4')}** | 36+ (Garden of Stone, floor 16 down) | {formatCoins(itemsById.get('war_horn_4').basePrice)} |

War horns can be traded between players and sold to merchants, so you can
simply buy a grade you can't farm yourself. Difficulty follows the **war
horn's grade**, not your estate's location — wave monster levels and
to-hit bonuses mirror the matching Garden of Stone floors.

## Conditions before blowing it

Using a war horn on clear ground on your own estate (or a family estate
where you're the **head**) runs a list of server checks; failing any of
them tells you why and leaves the horn unused:

- You and the banner spot must be on the same estate; only the family
  **head** may blow the horn on a family estate — a member who finds one
  has to hand it to the head or drop it in the family chest.
- That estate must have no unpaid tax.
- You must be standing on ground floor, outdoors — not on water, indoors,
  on a roof, or on a second floor.
- **A 10m clear radius around the banner spot** — fences, houses, and
  furniture (including family banners and storage chests) all count no
  matter who placed them; trees and terrain don't. Failing this tells you
  where the nearest obstruction is.
- That estate can't already have a defense running — only one at a time
  per estate.
- At least three of the four compass directions around the clear circle
  need a valid monster spawn point.
- You can't be in combat, dead, or have a pending trade.
- It has to be **night** (sunset to sunrise, checked once against the
  server's sun position at the moment you blow it).

## The five waves

Blowing the horn consumes it and plants a **defense banner** 1m in front of
you (showing your family's emblem on a family estate), then runs a fixed
timetable:

| Time | Event |
|---|---|
| 0:00 | Horn blown, banner planted, alarm sent to the family and the summoner's party |
| 0:30 | Wave 1 (one direction) |
| 1:50 | Wave 2 (one direction, rotating clockwise) |
| 3:10 | Wave 3 |
| 4:30 | Wave 4 |
| 5:50 | Wave 5 — all four directions at once, with the leader mixed in |
| 7:00 | Earliest possible success check |
| 10:00 | Any monster still standing at this point means the defense failed |

Each wave stands in a pillar of light and low fog for two seconds before
marching toward the banner, giving you time to read which direction it's
coming from. Monster count is **fixed regardless of how many people show
up** — 6 per wave for waves 1–4, plus 3 per direction and one leader for
wave 5, 37 monsters total. The leader is drawn a third larger than an
ordinary monster.

The banner has **30 durability**: a raider with nothing to fight swings at
it on its own attack cooldown, 1 point per ordinary monster hit, 3 for the
leader. Any of the following fails the defense; the horn still counts as
used and kills already earned pay out, but there's no success reward:

- The banner's durability reaches 0.
- **A wipe** — no eligible participant alive within 30m of the banner for
  10 straight seconds (checked from wave 1 onward).
- Ten minutes pass with any wave monster still alive.

## Rewards for holding it

**Kill rewards are identical to any ordinary monster** — XP split by damage
share, loot from the usual drop tables — except wave monsters never drop a
war horn themselves. Clearing all five waves, with the check happening no
earlier than 7:00, counts as a win:

- **Eligibility**: the summoner, family members on a family estate, and
  the summoner's party. Anyone passing by can still fight and still earns
  kill rewards — they just don't qualify for the rewards below.
- **Threshold**: alive within 30m of the banner at the moment of success,
  having dealt damage totaling at least **twice that grade's average wave
  monster HP**.
- **The chest**: rises where the banner stood, split into one share per
  qualifying participant. Each share runs roughly 3 healing potions, one
  grade-matched Defender's Purse, one return-type scroll, a chance at a
  weapon or armor enchant scroll (10% each), a Phoenix Talisman (5%), and
  a smaller chance at grade-appropriate gear. It opens on its own after 5
  minutes if nobody opens it.
- **A trophy**: the summoner additionally gets a wall trophy to hang on
  the estate — **{itemName('trophy_orc_axes')}**,
  **{itemName('trophy_cyclops_skull')}**, **{itemName('trophy_statue_face')}**,
  or **{itemName('trophy_serpent_head')}**, matching the horn's grade. It
  can't be traded or sold, and clicking it shows the defense record behind
  it.
- **A title**: a qualifying participant's first win at a given grade grants
  a matching title — see
  [Titles](../../guides/titles/#estate-defense-titles-and-disgrace).

## Defense failure: disgrace

Losing a defense hangs a "Who Lost the Banner" disgrace for **ten days**,
shown in the same spot a chosen title would be, covering whatever title was
showing (your pick isn't lost — it comes back once disgrace expires).

- On a family estate, the loss disgraces the **whole family** (online or
  not, including anyone who joins within the ten days); on an estate with
  no family, only the summoner is disgraced.
- Failing again while already disgraced restarts the ten-day count from
  that moment — it doesn't stack.
- Blowing the horn again while disgraced and winning washes the disgrace
  away immediately, without waiting out the full ten days.

Admins can open a practice run at their own position with `/warhorn N`
(N being the grade), skipping the estate, night, and horn-consumption
checks. The chest still appears, but no title is granted and no disgrace
record is kept.

## agent-client (bot players)

There's no special notification format for this yet — bots follow exactly
the same rules as human players: find a war horn, find clear ground, use it
at night. Combat and the win/loss checks work identically, with no separate
rally or auto-engage mechanism.
