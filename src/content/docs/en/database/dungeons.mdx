export const signature = (d) => (d.chestDrops.length ? d.chestDrops.map(itemName).join(', ') : '—');
export const claimedItemIds = new Set(
  dungeons.flatMap((d) => [...d.chestDrops, ...d.chestRolls.map((r) => r.itemId)])
);

**{dungeons.length} dungeons** are open. Dungeons are **seed-determined**: the
same entrance always has the same layout and is never regenerated.

The **Orc Warlord** is a step up: its 120 health is set directly in the data
(not the 45 the level would derive), with Guard 13, +6 to hit and 2d8 damage.
Orc Warrens runs 10 floors, so put a full leather set together before going
down.

**Barkan** (the Sundered Bastion boss) casts a rune slash in place of a
plain swing starting 6s into a fight and every 15s after: he locks his
facing and holds still through the cast, ignoring staggers, with raising
the sword as the only tell. At the 2.55s mark the blade sweeps and every
player in the 90° × 4m fan in front takes 5x a plain hit with no guard
roll; stepping out of the fan before the sweep avoids it, and he's open
for 2.8s after the swing for a counter. Admins have a `/god` test command
that takes no damage, connection-local only, invisible to regular players.

**The Thief King** (Thieves' Den) and the **Shadow Master** (Shadow Guild)
share the same attack timing: instead of the plain 2.8s slash, both use the
player's dagger-stab animation every 2s, still for 3d6 damage.

A slain boss doesn't respawn on a timer — its floor holds it dead until the
next **sunset dungeon reset** (once per game day), which also teleports
everyone still in that dungeon back to its entrance with a distant roar.
Opening the chest no longer requires killing the boss or standing next to it
— see "Keys and locked floors" below. The weapon each boss used to drop
itself (Goblin Sword, Iron Sword, Steel Longsword, Cutthroat Dirk) is now a
guaranteed chest drop instead; see the table below.

## Keys and locked floors

Every **5th floor**'s stair-room exit has a locked door that always exists,
blocking the way on completely:

<FilterTable
  placeholder="Search dungeons…"
  columns={[
    { key: 'name', label: 'Dungeon' },
    { key: 'floors', label: 'Locked floors' },
    { key: 'chest', label: 'Key for the chest' },
  ]}
  rows={dungeons
    .filter((d) => d.keyPrefix)
    .map((d) => {
      const locked = lockedFloors(d);
      return {
        name: d.name,
        floors: locked.map((f) => `Floor ${f}`).join(', '),
        chest: locked.map((f) => itemName(`${d.keyPrefix}_${f}`)).join(', '),
      };
    })}
/>

- **Keys drop in the section just before their locked door** (for example,
  Orc Warrens floors 1–4 drop the floor-5 key, floors 6–9 drop the floor-10
  key). Monsters killed in that section have a **5%** chance to drop the
  matching key; breaking a barrel, crate or clutter chest there is **1%**. A
  key from a kill goes to whoever dealt the most damage; a key from a broken
  barrel or crate goes to whoever broke it (see [Party system](../../guides/party/#kill-loot-ownership)).
  The roll is skipped once a character already holds that key, or has
  already opened that dungeon's chest tonight.
- Once a key hits the ground, whoever has first claim gets a 30-second
  exclusive pickup window before anyone else can take it; **a floor-5 key
  won't open a floor-10 door.**
- Holding the key and clicking the locked door opens it. **The door doesn't
  consume the key** — dying or leaving and coming back still works, and keys
  never expire. An opened door **stays open for 30 seconds** before
  auto-closing, so someone without a key can slip in behind you during that
  window, but once it closes they're stuck on whichever side they ended up
  on.
- **Opening the chest needs every locked floor's key for that dungeon** (see
  the "Key for the chest" column above) — missing even one blocks it, and
  opening the chest spends all of them at once. The boss still guards the
  chest room, so opening it safely usually still means dealing with it.
  Only the deepest door's key used to be required, with earlier-section keys
  merely "consumed if you happened to be carrying one"; as of 2026-11-01
  every key type is required, which also retires the old exception where "a
  key you yourself used to open a door tonight is required at the chest"
  (fixed 2026-10-23) — that exception has nothing left to except now that
  every key is mandatory anyway.
- Keys are **tradeable** — selling one sells the buyer the right to open
  tonight's chest with it, so a character too weak to clear a section can
  still buy their way in.

## Chest drop rules

Each dungeon's chest drops come in a few parts:

1. **Signature drop(s)** — one or two guaranteed items per dungeon, the
   "Guaranteed drop" column above; several boss weapons now count among
   them.
2. **Exclusive rolls** — only that dungeon's own chest rolls these, at the
   chance listed below. This layer was added once tiers 4 and 5 each got a
   second dungeon: tier-pool items would otherwise be shared by both
   dungeons of the same tier, and Sundered Bastion's Rune Blade and Amulet
   of the Earth were meant to stay exclusive to it rather than also showing
   up in Shadow Guild. The chances didn't change.
3. **Tier-pool independent rolls** — the equipment pool shared by dungeons of
   that tier (below), minus anything already counted as a signature or
   exclusive-roll item; every remaining piece rolls separately at its own
   chance.
4. **Lower-tier carry-over** — equipment from tiers below the dungeon each get
   an extra 10% roll, so you can fill gaps in a higher dungeon.

Gold is `depth × 500` to `depth × 1500`.

The target is roughly **five runs on average** to collect everything a tier
introduces.

<FilterTable

    { key: 'name', label: 'Equipment' },

    .map((i) => ({
      name: i.name,
      tier: `T${i.chestTier}`,
      chance: i.chestChance ? `${Math.round(i.chestChance * 100)}%` : 'Guaranteed',
      guard: i.guard ?? '—',
      cha: i.chaBonus ? `+${i.chaBonus}` : '—',
      effect: i.hasSustenance ? 'Satiation drain ×0.75' : '—',
    }))}
/>

:::note[The only weapons in chests are boss weapons — plus Shadowfang]
Weapons are normally obtained from "the monster carrying it drops it" — the
exception is the Goblin Sword, Iron Sword, Steel Longsword and Cutthroat
Dirk, which are now guaranteed chest drops instead of boss drops (the Thief
King and Shadow Master both fight with a generic dagger animation, so
killing them doesn't drop Cutthroat Dirk or Shadowfang). **Shadowfang**
doesn't fit that rule either — it isn't any boss's weapon, it's purely
Shadow Guild's exclusive chest roll (see the table above), at 20%. Every
other weapon still works the old way: what you see a monster carrying is
what it can drop, and chests don't carry it.
:::

## Other things down there

- **Barrels and crates break** — click one and your character walks up and
  swings once. Treasure chests don't break.
- **Chests are opened** — click one and your character walks over and lifts the
  lid. A real coin pile drops in front of it; picking it up credits 1–10 copper
  straight to your wallet without taking a bag slot.
- **Corridors have doors** — where a corridor opens into a room there's a
  clickable wooden double door.
- **Corridor and room walls differ** — rooms use medieval stone, corridors use
  rock. Useful for telling where you are.
