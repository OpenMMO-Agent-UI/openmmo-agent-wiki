## The catch table

`catchWeight` is the relative weight at fishing level 0 — bigger means more
often.

## How the odds work

- **Two independent pools**: flotsam holds a flat **20%** of the draw at every
  level. No amount of skill thins out the old boots.
- **Skill is multiplicative**: a fish's weight grows **3%** per level per point
  of rarity. That closes the gap on rare fish but **can never invert the
  order** — a Golden Sturgeon will never be more common than a Minnow.
- **Level gates**: River Salmon needs fishing level **10**, Golden Sturgeon
  needs **20**. Below that they don't appear at all.

## Size and trophies

Length is the `size dice` roll plus a d20 quality roll. **A natural 20 doubles
the size**, and for fish it's always a trophy.

Trophies are a fish concept — a nat-20 Old Boot is just a very large boot, no
celebration.

Size is **not stored on the item**; it exists only in the catch announcement.

## Is it worth the time?

The expected **sell** value of one catch runs about **8 copper** at level 0 to
about **24 copper** at the level-30 cap. For comparison:

- Coin piles: 1–10 copper
- An NPC's daily wage in this economy: 50 silver

So an hour of active fishing is roughly half a guard's daily salary.
**Steady pocket money, not a money printer** — that's deliberate, and a
contract test pins the per-catch expected value between 5 and 25 copper while
also proving that levelling never makes you poorer and the cap never earns more
than 4× level 0.

The Golden Sturgeon is the jackpot: it sells for
{formatCoins(items.find((i) => i.id === 'golden_sturgeon').basePrice)} — a
goblin sword's worth — but it's only about 1.7% of draws even at the level-30
cap.

## Fish are edible, but not raw

Fish are both **food** and **healing items**:

    };
  })}
/>

:::caution[Raw fish cause food poisoning]
Raw fish give a fixed **40** nutrition regardless of species, and carry a
**food poisoning risk**: five minutes of move speed ×0.6, attack speed ×0.6,
max carry ×0.6, satiation draining 4× faster and HP regen stopped. These
**multiply** with your current state — even Well-Fed leaves you moving at ×0.66.

Eating raw is an emergency measure. The answer is **a campfire**. Grilled fish
carry no risk and far more nutrition.
:::

Every catch takes its own bag slot (they don't stack), so your bag reads as a
catch log.
