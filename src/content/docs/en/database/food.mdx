Your character carries a **satiation** value from 0 to 1000. New characters
start at **700**.

- **Decay**: 1 per 20 seconds while online. **It does not decay offline.**
  Wearing the **{itemName('silver_necklace')}** slows this to **0.75x**
  (sprinting included).
- **Recovery**: eating adds the item's nutrition, capped at 1000.
- **You cannot starve to death**: it stops at 0 and only the Weak penalties
  remain. Respawning after death resets it to 300.

One game day is three real hours. The tuning anchor is **one meal per game
day**.

## The bands

The system is framed as a **buff**: the baseline is ordinary, being well fed
earns a bonus, and penalties only exist at the extremes.

| State | Range | Move speed | Attack speed | Max carry | HP regen |
|---|---|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| Stuffed | 851–1000 | — | — | — | — |
| **Well-Fed** | **300–850** | **×1.1** | **×1.1** | **×1.15** | — |
| Hungry | 100–300 | — | — | — | — |
| Weak | 0–100 | ×0.75 | ×0.75 | ×0.6 | **stops** |

**You should spend most of your time Well-Fed** — monster difficulty and the
rest of the balance are tuned assuming it.

:::note[Stuffed isn't a penalty, but it isn't a bonus either]
Eating below 800 **clamps the result at 850**, so a single piece of jerky can't
knock you out of Well-Fed; the excess is simply discarded. Only eating **at 800
or above** pushes the overflow through into Stuffed, which digests back down.
At 1000 you can't eat at all.
:::

The carry multiplier applies to `STR × 15`. Losing the Well-Fed buff or going
Weak can put you over the limit — that **doesn't stop you moving**, it just
refuses new pickups and purchases.

## What to carry

The two efficiency columns above answer it:

- **Nutrition per kg** — the bag has a weight limit, so this is the column for
  long trips.
- **Nutrition per copper** — the column for when money is tight.

Among shop food,
**{(() => { const b = foods.filter(f => rica.catalog.includes(f.id)).sort((a,b) => (b.nutrition/b.weight) - (a.nutrition/a.weight))[0]; return b.name; })()}**
is the most weight-efficient and
**{(() => { const b = foods.filter(f => rica.catalog.includes(f.id) && f.basePrice).sort((a,b) => (b.nutrition/b.basePrice) - (a.nutrition/a.basePrice))[0]; return b.name; })()}**
is the most cost-efficient.

Grilled fish generally beat shop food — but you have to catch them and you need
a fire.

## Campfires and grilling

A **{itemName('campfire_kit')}**
({formatCoins(items.find((i) => i.id === 'campfire_kit').basePrice)},
{items.find((i) => i.id === 'campfire_kit').weight} kg) lights a fire in the
field and burns for **ten minutes**.

Grilling a fish removes the poisoning risk, raises the nutrition sharply, and
raises the sale price.

Food **does not restore health** — that's the potion's job. Fish are the
exception: they keep their healing effect as well (see [Fishing](../fishing/)).

## Food poisoning

Eating raw fish can cause it. It lasts **five minutes**:

| Effect | Multiplier |
|---|:---:|
| Move speed | ×0.6 |
| Attack speed | ×0.6 |
| Max carry | ×0.6 |
| Satiation decay | **4× faster** |
| HP regen | **stops** |

These **multiply** with your current satiation state: Well-Fed still leaves you
at ×0.66 (1.1 × 0.6), and Weak at ×0.45. Re-infection only refreshes the
duration; it doesn't stack. **It deals no damage** — consistent with the rule
that hunger never kills you.
